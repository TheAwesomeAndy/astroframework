## What is mathematically exact versus empirically unestablished

Exact within a selected formal model:

- zodiacal coordinates supplied/calculated under the named provider;
- Whole Sign placement;
- admitted aspect geometry;
- ruler/dispositor edges;
- SCCs/basins/routes;
- condition-rule outputs;
- typed hyperedge detection under its registry/orb policy;
- statistics computed under a named counterfactual.

Not established merely by those calculations:

- psychological or spiritual primacy;
- predictive life-event efficacy;
- causal planetary forces;
- measured physical-energy fields;
- real-population rarity;
- superiority to expert astrological practice.

## Energy / field language

Terms such as energy, current, field, resonance, compression, expansion, friction, coherence, standing-wave polarity, or quadrature torque are symbolic/phenomenological interpretive vocabulary unless a separate physical model and evidence are supplied.

Angular geometry is computational. Interpretive field language is downstream.

## Current research ceiling

Allowed:

- detected under named formal rules;
- derivationally verified;
- null-tested under named artificial counterfactuals;
- empirical percentile inside a simulated reference distribution;
- departure/no-departure under a declared FDR family.

Withheld:

- real natal population frequency;
- phenotype association;
- causal inference;
- validated prediction;
- spiritual proof.
