# Noetic Atlas Research Constitution

## North star

Noetic Atlas is both an astrological instrument for human understanding and an experimental apparatus for investigating astrology itself.

The consumer application is necessary: it keeps the work intelligible, useful, grounded in lived experience, and economically sustainable. The research program is the deeper reason for the instrument to exist: to formalize inherited knowledge, test contested extensions, recover forgotten techniques, and search for structures that have not yet been named.

The governing identity is:

```text
radical curiosity + strict provenance
```

And the constitutional requirement above all others is:

> **Noetic Atlas must remain capable of surprising its creators.**

If every result is something the builders already knew to request, the system is a lookup engine rather than a research instrument.

---

## Three research regimes

### Operational

Operational models contain reproducible techniques inside an explicitly named astrological tradition or production contract. They are the control state used by the default Personal aperture.

Operational does **not** mean experimentally proven physical law. It means the rules are sufficiently explicit to calculate reproducibly under the named model.

Examples include the current Hellenistic/traditional substrate: tropical coordinates, Whole-Sign houses, traditional domicile rulership, the canonical major-aspect family, lots, sect, primitive condition, relational condition, compound condition, dispositor topology, and House River routing.

**Contamination rule:** Experimental and Discovery objects may not silently rewrite Operational objects.

### Experimental

Experimental models are named, versioned hypotheses. They may alter, extend, or compare a model, but the change must be explicit and reversible.

Examples can include, without being limited to:

- modern outer co-rulership models;
- alternative or expanded aspect families and orb contracts;
- Ceres as a Taurus significator, secondary ruler, co-ruler, or alternative ruler;
- other minor-body or calculated-point rulership/significator hypotheses;
- alternate lots or historical variants;
- alternate dignity or reception definitions;
- new field/interaction equations;
- alternative configuration definitions;
- historical model reconstructions from Hellenistic, Persian/Arabic, Medieval, Renaissance, Jyotish, Tibetan, or other traditions.

Ceres–Taurus is a **seed example**, not the center or boundary of the research program.

Every experimental result must carry a model identity and, where applicable, a `hypothesis_id`.

### Discovery

Discovery is open search for mathematically reproducible structures that may not exist in the hypothesis registry or inherited astrological vocabulary.

A Discovery candidate may be assigned a provisional identifier such as:

```text
NAF-CANDIDATE-0042
```

before anyone knows whether the structure has astrological significance or deserves a human name.

Discovery may search across:

- graph topology;
- multilayer intersections;
- hypergraph configurations;
- condition-qualified motifs;
- basin partitions;
- House River convergence;
- resonance × condition interaction;
- supplemental-aspect bridges;
- cross-chart multiplex structure;
- orb persistence;
- information-theoretic quantities;
- spectral/network quantities;
- temporal activation once the natal research substrate is mature;
- new mathematical representations that have not yet been developed.

**Detection is not significance.** A mathematically real structure is an invitation to investigate, not permission to declare a new astrological doctrine.

## Constitutional principles

1. **One astronomical state, many explicit models.**
2. **Tradition is prior knowledge, not unquestionable truth.**
3. **Experimental models never silently modify Operational models.**
4. **Mathematical detection precedes interpretation.**
5. **Structural novelty is not evidence of significance.**
6. **Every research claim carries provenance, assumptions, model identity, and epistemic regime.**
7. **Null comparison precedes rarity language.**
8. **The absence of an existing astrological name does not imply the absence of a real structure.**
9. **The presence of a mathematically identifiable structure does not imply astrological meaning.**
10. **The instrument exists both to illuminate inherited astrology and to make previously invisible questions askable.**
11. **Noetic Atlas must remain capable of surprising its creators.**

---

## Research lifecycle

Every Experimental or Discovery program should progress through:

```text
Detect → Describe → Compare → Test → Replicate → Interpret
```

Interpretation comes last.

A finding should be able to carry independent status for:

```text
geometry
derivation
historical analogue
population frequency
null comparison
phenomenological association
replication
interpretation
```

These statuses must not be collapsed into one confidence or strength number.

## Research Hypothesis Packs

A named hypothesis is represented as a machine-readable Research Hypothesis Pack with at least:

- `hypothesis_id`;
- proposition;
- Operational control model;
- explicit variants;
- expected structural effects;
- execution status;
- source/provenance state;
- cohort state;
- null-model state;
- replication state;
- interpretation state.

A registered hypothesis that cannot yet be executed must say so. `registered-not-executed` is not a computed result.

Hypothesis Packs are intentionally generic. A future experiment involving a newly discovered body, an unfamiliar aspect family, a historical Jyotish rule, a novel field equation, or an unnamed graph configuration should use the same research machinery.

---

## Model comparison

Given one deterministic chart state, Noetic Atlas may compare an Operational model with an Experimental variant:

\[
\Delta(\mathcal M_O,\mathcal M_E)
\]

The comparison asks what actually changed, for example:

- dispositor edges;
- terminal SCCs and basin membership;
- House River routes;
- bottlenecks and articulation structure;
- condition interactions;
- aspect/hypergraph motif membership;
- cross-layer detections;
- auditable Reading claims.

A structural delta does not determine which model is true. It only makes the consequences of the hypothesis visible and reproducible.

## Personal and Research apertures

Both apertures consume the same chart authority.

### Personal

Default to the Operational model. Present a coherent, auditable chart that helps a person understand natal architecture, conditioned routing, current life context once timing is implemented, and downstream phenomenological implications without exposing an unnecessary laboratory control panel.

### Research

Expose model identities, hypothesis packs, alternative policies, model deltas, Discovery candidates, status vectors, null/cohort state, and research provenance.

Different aperture. Same state.

---

## Energy / field language

Noetic Atlas is allowed to formalize astrology using field-oriented language such as resonance, phase, coupling, interference, compression, coherence, attractors, frequency, vibration, and energy.

At present these terms are mathematical/phenomenological model language unless an independently measured physical quantity is explicitly supplied. The system must not silently upgrade a symbolic field model into a laboratory-confirmed planetary force.

The research question is not "physics has already proven astrology." The question is whether rigorous mathematical, informational, dynamical, historical, and phenomenological models can reveal stable structure in the symbolic system humans call astrology, and whether those structures survive comparison, null testing, replication, and real-world investigation.

---

## Discovery and historical recovery

Research beyond the conventional includes both future novelty and recovered antiquity.

A structure may be new because nobody has identified it before, or it may be "new" to modern practice because an older tradition preserved it under another vocabulary. Noetic Atlas should therefore be able to ask whether techniques from different traditions are mathematically equivalent, partially overlapping, or genuinely different.

Historical scholarship and mathematical discovery belong in the same laboratory, provided their model identities remain explicit.

---

## Final standard

Noetic Atlas should make the known clearer, the contested testable, and the unknown findable.

It should never confuse curiosity with proof, and it should never confuse rigor with intellectual closure.
