# Noetic Atlas Research Constitution

Status: living constitutional doctrine through v0.4.7.

## North star

Noetic Atlas is both an astrological instrument and an experimental apparatus for investigating formal astrology.

**Consumer utility is the application. Discovery is the research program.**

The governing combination is **radical curiosity under strict provenance**.

> **Noetic Atlas must remain capable of surprising its creators.**

## Frozen architectural law

```text
one astronomical/chart state → many explicit models → many coordinated projections
```

A view or experiment may not independently recalculate or silently replace the astronomical/kernel substrate.

## Three regimes

### Operational

Reproducible techniques inside an explicitly named astrological model. Operational means well-defined and reproducible within that model; it does **not** mean experimentally proven as physical law.

Personal mode uses Operational state by default.

### Experimental

Named/versioned hypotheses evaluated against an Operational control. Every Experimental result must carry hypothesis/model identity, assumptions, provenance, and a reversible comparison. It never overwrites Operational state.

### Discovery

Open search for unnamed but mathematically reproducible structures. Detection precedes naming. Counterfactual comparison precedes unexpectedness language. Population baselines precede population-rarity language. Interpretation follows evidence rather than preceding it.

## Constitutional principles

1. **One astronomical state, many explicit models.**
2. **Tradition is prior knowledge, not unquestionable truth.**
3. **Experimental models never silently modify Operational models.**
4. **Mathematical detection precedes interpretation.**
5. **Structural novelty is not evidence of significance.**
6. **Every research claim carries provenance, assumptions, and model identity.**
7. **Named counterfactual comparison precedes unexpectedness language.**
8. **Artificial null departure is not real-population rarity.**
9. **The absence of an inherited astrological name does not imply the absence of a reproducible structure.**
10. **The presence of a reproducible structure does not imply astrological meaning.**
11. **Independent epistemic dimensions may not be collapsed into a confidence/strength score.**
12. **Negative results remain research results.**
13. **Public release requires live product verification, not only merged code.**

## Counterfactual doctrine

A null model is a question, not a neutral universe.

Every null must declare:

- what it preserves;
- what it randomizes;
- what statistic it evaluates;
- what question it can answer;
- what it cannot establish;
- generator limitations/quality.

Current named nulls are N_G, N_L, N_D, and N_T.

There is no valid constitutional concept of “3/4 nulls passed.” Different nulls are not pseudo-replications.

## Population doctrine

Until a real empirical cohort/reference layer exists:

```text
population_frequency = unknown
```

Words such as `rare among people`, `uncommon in natal charts`, or `population-enriched` are outside the claim ceiling.

The planned v0.5.0 Population Cohort Engine is the proper owner of real prevalence questions.

## Interpretation doctrine

Interpretation is downstream.

A geometric, topological, condition, hypergraph, or null result may inform an interpretation only through an explicitly labeled interpretive model. It does not become psychological, spiritual, medical, predictive, or causal merely because the mathematical object is exact.

## Field-language boundary

Noetic Atlas may formalize resonance, phase, coupling, interference, persistence, frequency, vibration, or energy as mathematical/phenomenological research language. Such terms are not presented as laboratory-confirmed planetary physical forces without independent physical evidence.

## Historical recovery

Hellenistic, Persian/Arabic, Medieval, Jyotish, Renaissance, and other traditions may be encoded as parallel source-locked models. Apparent equivalence across traditions is a hypothesis to test, not permission to collapse terminology or provenance.

## Consumer / research separation

Personal mode emphasizes Operational chart structure, Reading, House Flow, condition, resonance, and proof.

Research mode exposes hypothesis identities, Discovery candidates, named nulls, hyperedges, research-status vectors, and provenance.

Both consume the same chart authority.

No Experimental or Discovery object enters the Operational model by popularity, visual novelty, interpretive appeal, or one attractive canonical chart.

Promotion requires an explicit decision supported by the comparison, population, replication, validation, and source/model evidence appropriate to the claim.
