# Noetic Atlas v0.4.1.1 — Interpretive Analysis and Outer-Planet Participation

## Purpose

v0.4.1 established a graph-analysis and explainable-finding layer. It still left a product-level gap: a user could see that a T-square, terminal SCC, bottleneck, bridge, or central node existed without receiving a sufficiently developed astrological delineation of what that structure means inside the chart.

v0.4.1.1 introduces a separate downstream **Astrological Analysis Engine**.

The pipeline is now:

```text
Input / astronomy
→ astrological rules
→ graph derivation
→ graph findings / metrics
→ astrological interpretation
→ material-life translation
```

Interpretation never replaces the lower layers. Every analysis object retains the placements, aspects, graph measurements, rule identifiers, and ledger references used to produce it.

## Epistemic layer

Interpretive analysis is explicitly labeled:

```text
interpretive-inference
```

It is not labeled astronomical computation, mathematical derivation, or empirical validation.

The engine may synthesize deterministic chart facts into an astrological hypothesis. It may not present that synthesis as a measured physical effect or demonstrated causal fact.

## Outer planets

Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto now participate fully in the product's astrological analysis.

They are analyzed through:

- sign placement;
- Whole-Sign house placement;
- admitted major aspects and orbs;
- applying/separating status when available;
- degree in the aspect graph;
- normalized betweenness;
- articulation-point status;
- participation in recognized typed motifs;
- house-domain synthesis;
- transpersonal/modern planetary archetype.

They **do not inherit Hellenistic essential dignity**. Therefore an outer planet may be richly interpreted while still being `not_applicable` to the classical condition engine.

This distinction is intentional:

```text
outer-planet analysis = applicable
Hellenistic dignity for outer planet = not applicable
```

## Placement analysis

Every planetary placement can generate an interpretive record with:

1. planetary function;
2. sign field;
3. Whole-Sign house domain;
4. admitted major aspects;
5. tight-contact summary;
6. life/material expression;
7. evidence object;
8. interpretation-rule IDs and ledger references.

The house is used as the primary bridge from symbolic description to lived domain. This prevents a placement from being reduced to a personality adjective.

## Aspect analysis

Major aspects use explicit angular mechanics:

```text
0°   conjunction = phase fusion
60°  sextile     = catalytic compatibility
90°  square      = quadrature / cross-purpose torque
120° trine       = low-impedance phase coherence
180° opposition  = standing-wave polarity
```

The interpretation then identifies:

- both planetary functions;
- both house domains;
- orb and phase;
- the type of interaction implied by the selected astrological model;
- a practical/lived question generated by the relationship.

## T-square analysis

A detected T-square is no longer rendered as only a motif label.

The interpretive engine derives:

- the opposition spine;
- the two polar planets;
- their signs and houses;
- the apex planet;
- the apex sign and house;
- the two square edges;
- the apex as the geometric release point of the configuration;
- the feedback loop between opposition pressure and apex action;
- the concrete house domains through which the configuration may become visible.

The analysis asks how the apex planetary function can carry both ends of the opposition rather than simply describing the configuration as "tension."

## Grand Trine analysis

A Grand Trine is treated as a closed three-node 120° coherence circuit rather than three unrelated trines.

The interpretation distinguishes:

- mathematical motif closure;
- the three planetary functions;
- their signs and houses;
- the low-impedance character of the three paths;
- the difference between available coherence and conscious development;
- the material domains through which the circuit can be given an external task.

## Topology analysis

Graph topology now has a downstream delineation layer.

### Terminal SCC / basin

The system first states the exact routing fact, then interprets:

- the planets inside the terminal SCC;
- their signs and houses;
- the size of the basin feeding the SCC;
- primitive condition of the terminal classical planets;
- the material domains where the terminal circuit may become observable.

The engine explicitly refuses to translate terminality into an unsupported claim that the terminal planets are automatically the "strongest" or "most important."

### Rulership bottleneck

A nonterminal bottleneck analysis explains:

- how many upstream routes pass through the node;
- where it lies before terminal entry;
- the planetary function of that node;
- its sign and house;
- why it can be treated as a shared transformation step in multiple rulership chains.

Again, the graph fact is narrower than a global dominance claim.

## Life / material expression

Every user-facing interpretive card contains a dedicated **Life / material expression** block.

This section translates the symbolic configuration into the involved Whole-Sign house domains. It is designed to make the system useful to a non-specialist without claiming deterministic fate.

Examples of domains include resources, career, children/creation, partnerships, home/family, work/routines, higher learning, communities, shared resources, and withdrawal/private processes.

## Integrity

Every interpretation object contains:

```text
model
kind
epistemic_layer
rules[]
inputs
ledger_refs[]
statement / limitation
```

The user can open **Why this analysis?** from the Analysis surface and inspect the evidence object and interpretation provenance.

The intended hierarchy is:

```text
Analysis       = readable delineation
Findings       = graph-derived structural claims
Metrics        = quantitative graph measurements
Condition      = classical rule-defined planetary condition
Integrity      = proof and provenance
```

The public interface therefore defaults to **Analysis**, not Metrics.

## Current limits

v0.4.1.1 is a first interpretive substrate, not a finished delineation corpus.

Its language is deterministic and rule-based so that future sample analyses can improve the interpretation layer without altering astronomy or graph mathematics.

Still pending:

- a richer corpus of expert-written delineation examples;
- source/version registry for distinct interpretive schools;
- reception and overcoming;
- compound traditional condition;
- house-ruler synthesis beyond the current topology analysis;
- aspect-pattern synthesis across overlapping motifs;
- explicit treatment of nodes/lots/angles in the interpretive engine;
- temporal activation / Life Spectrum;
- user-selectable interpretation posture (Traditional / Psychological / Transpersonal / Exploratory / Mystical);
- null-model baselines for research claims of unusual graph organization.

## Training direction

Future sample analyses supplied by the project owner should be treated as an interpretation corpus, not as replacements for deterministic calculation.

The preferred evolution is:

```text
stable chart facts
+ stable graph facts
+ versioned interpretation rules/corpus
= richer auditable analysis
```

This allows Noetic Atlas to become more sophisticated in delineation without sacrificing the project's central integrity principle: the user must always be able to distinguish what was calculated, what was derived, and what was interpreted.
